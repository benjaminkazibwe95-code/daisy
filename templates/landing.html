What she does

Everyday help, in one app.

Everything below is in the app today.

Homework, explained

Type a school question or snap a photo of it. Daisy walks through it step by step so you learn it, not just copy it.

Start a business

Tell her what you sell and where. She helps you plan it and can build a website so customers can find you.

Ask about any picture

A page from your book, a plant, a receipt. Add a photo and ask.

Talk instead of typing

Speak your question, or start a live voice conversation. Daisy can read every answer aloud.

Checks the web

For news, prices and scores, Daisy can search the internet. You can switch it off in Settings.

Talks the way you like

Choose direct, patient, warm or short, and Daisy carries it into every answer.

Installs like an app

Add it to your home screen from this page. No app store, and past chats stay readable offline.

Shared projects

Keep a project in one place and work on it together with others.

Your data, your control

Export your data, clear chat history, or make Daisy forget what she picked up, all from Settings.

FAQ

Good questions.

Is Daisy really free?

Yes. Daisy is free to start, with no card required. Daisy Max will have its own pricing, which we'll announce before charging anything.

Do I need an app store?

No. Tap Download on this page and Daisy installs on your home screen like any app. On iPhone, use Safari's Share menu and choose Add to Home Screen.

Can Daisy be wrong?

Yes. Like any AI, she can make mistakes. Check important information before relying on it.

What happens to my chats?

Your messages are used to run Daisy and, as our Privacy Policy explains, to improve our models. We don't sell personal information. You can clear history, export your data or ask us to delete your account at any time.

Does Daisy need internet?

Yes, for new messages. Past chats stay readable offline.

Who is behind Daisy?

TrustedBiz, a software company based in Entebbe, Uganda. You can reach us at hello@trustedbiz.co.ug.
