Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 11, 2026

Daisy is an AI assistant developed and operated by TrustedBiz. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use Daisy, how we use it, and the choices available to you. By using Daisy, you agree to the practices described here.

Daisy is offered in two versions. Daisy is our standard assistant, built on our Beni model. Daisy Max is our advanced assistant with additional capabilities including voice, built on our Beni 2 model. Both versions are covered by this Privacy Policy unless stated otherwise.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information. If you create an account to use Daisy, we collect your name, email address, and a password (stored only as a one-way hash, never in plain text). Using Daisy as a guest needs no account.

Messages and Conversations. When you chat with Daisy, we collect the messages you send and the responses Daisy generates in return. This is the core information Daisy needs to function, since every response is generated based on what you type or say.

Settings and Preferences. Daisy allows you to customize how it responds, including persona instructions and whether Daisy is allowed to search the internet for current information. These preferences are saved so Daisy remembers them the next time you open the app.

Feedback. If you rate a response, report a problem, or contact our support team, we collect the content of that feedback along with any related conversation needed to understand and act on it.

Information Collected Automatically. When you use Daisy, our systems automatically collect certain technical information, including your device type, operating system, app version, general location derived from your network connection, and log information that helps us understand crashes or errors.

Voice and microphone. If you use voice typing or the Live Room, the app asks for microphone permission. Speech is turned into text by your phone's built-in speech recognition (on Android this is provided by Google). Daisy does not record or store audio. Only the resulting text is sent to Daisy like any typed message.

Spoken answers. To speak answers in a natural voice, the text of Daisy's reply is sent to our server, which uses Microsoft's neural text-to-speech voices to create the audio. Only the text of Daisy's own reply is sent for this, not your account details.

Photos and files you choose. If you attach a photo, it is sent with your message so Daisy can look at it. The app only accesses photos you pick or take.

Documents and scanning. If you attach a document (PDF, Word, text) or a photo, its content is sent with your message to our server and to our AI provider (Anthropic) so Daisy can read it and answer. We do not keep the file after the answer is produced. The document scanner is provided by Google Play services and runs on your phone; scanned pages are saved only on your phone (Downloads/Daisy) unless you attach them to a chat.

AI provider. To generate answers, the text of your messages (and any photo or document you attach) is processed by Anthropic's Claude models under their API terms. Please do not attach files you are not comfortable sharing.

Contacts (optional). If you ask Daisy to call or message someone by name, the app can ask permission to read your contacts. The lookup happens on your phone to find a phone number. Your contacts are not uploaded or stored by us. Calls and messages are never sent without you pressing the final button in your phone's own Phone, Messages or WhatsApp app.

Notifications. If you allow notifications, the app shows a "Daisy is live" notice while the Live Room is open.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and operate Daisy, including generating responses to your messages
  • Remember your settings and preferences between sessions
  • Maintain conversation history so Daisy can refer back to earlier parts of a conversation
  • Diagnose technical issues and improve the reliability of the app
  • Respond to support requests and feedback
  • Improve Daisy's models, including Beni and Beni 2, so future responses are more accurate and useful
  • Detect misuse of the service and enforce our Terms of Use
  • Meet legal obligations where applicable

We do not use your information to make automated decisions that have a legal or similarly significant effect on you.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with service providers who help us host our infrastructure and deliver Daisy to you, such as cloud hosting providers and the voice provider described above. If you use the more advanced assistant, your messages may be processed by an AI provider acting on our behalf to produce a reply. We may disclose information if required by law, such as in response to a valid legal request from a court or government authority. We may share information to protect the rights, property, or safety of TrustedBiz, our users, or the public, including in cases of suspected fraud or abuse. If TrustedBiz is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Privacy Policy or a policy at least as protective.

4. Data Retention

We retain your conversation history and account information for as long as your account remains active, so that Daisy can continue to provide a consistent experience across sessions. You may request deletion of your data at any time as described in Section 5. Once you request deletion, we will remove your data from active systems within a reasonable time, except where we are required to retain certain information for legal or security purposes.

Chats are stored on your phone. Chats you add to a shared project, and your account details, are stored on our server. Reports you send about an answer are kept so we can review and improve safety.

5. Your Rights and Choices, and Deleting Your Account

Delete your account and data. In the Daisy app go to Settings, then Account, then Delete my account. You can also use our web page without opening the app: daisy-qg1c.onrender.com/delete-account, or email hello@trustedbiz.co.ug from your account email with the subject "Delete my Daisy account". We delete your account, your projects and the chats saved on our server. We may keep limited records where the law requires it or to prevent fraud and abuse, and reports about answers may be kept in a form that no longer identifies you. Chats stored only on your phone are removed when you clear chats in Settings or uninstall the app.

You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you. You have the right to request correction of inaccurate information. You have the right to request deletion of your account and associated data. You have the right to withdraw consent for optional features, such as internet search, at any time through the app settings.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 9. We will respond within a reasonable time and may ask you to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

6. Children's Privacy

Daisy includes safeguards that block content that sexualises or endangers children, and users can report any answer from inside the app.

Daisy is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International Users
